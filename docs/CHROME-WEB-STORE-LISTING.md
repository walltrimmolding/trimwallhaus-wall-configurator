## Detailed description

Plan a balanced wall molding layout before you order materials or make a cut.

Wall Molding Calculator by Trim Wall Haus turns your wall dimensions into a proportional drawing, equal spacing, material estimate and practical cut list. Choose the design style that fits your room, enter the measurements and review the result immediately.

Choose from four calculators:

- Picture frame molding calculator for balanced decorative wall frames
- Board and batten calculator for even batten spacing and panel counts
- Accent wall calculator for square or rectangular grid layouts
- Wainscoting calculator for lower-wall panels and chair rail placement

Key features:

- Instant proportional wall preview
- Inch and centimetre measurements
- Equal-spacing calculations
- Material total with an adjustable waste allowance
- Printable cut list and Save as PDF workflow
- Live matching Trim Wall Haus kit price ranges
- Measurements saved locally on your device
- Optional free architectural drawing request
- Direct access to 11 installation-ready wall molding collections

The calculator is intended for project planning. Always verify the physical wall, corners, doors, windows, outlets and other obstructions before ordering or cutting material.

## Single-purpose statement

This extension calculates and displays wall molding layouts, spacing, material estimates and cut lists, with optional links to matching Trim Wall Haus products and an optional customer-initiated design request.

## Permission justification

`https://trimwallhaus.com/*` is requested only to retrieve live product pricing, open matching product collections and submit the contact form when the user explicitly requests a free architectural drawing. The extension does not read unrelated websites or browsing history.

## Data-use disclosure

- Locally stored: selected style, measurement unit and wall measurements, only when the user chooses Save measurements.
- Sent only after user action: name, email address, project note, measurements and calculated result when the user submits the free architectural drawing request.
- Live pricing request: does not include the user's name, email address, project note or wall measurements.
- Not collected: browsing history, authentication data, financial information, health information, precise location or unrelated website content.
- Data is not sold and is not used for advertising.
