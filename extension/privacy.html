Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the wall-molding, stair, paint, wall-area, wallpaper and baseboard calculator web tools and Chrome extension handle information.

Measurements stored locally

If you choose “Save measurements,” the selected wall design, unit and measurements are stored locally in your browser. They are not sent to Trim Wall Haus by the save feature. The utility calculators run locally and do not save their inputs. You can clear saved wall measurements by resetting the calculator or clearing browser storage.

Live product pricing

The calculator requests live product pricing from trimwallhaus.com so it can show relevant kit price ranges. This request does not include your wall measurements, name, email address or project note.

Free drawing request

Your name, email address, project note, measurements and calculated result are sent to the Trim Wall Haus contact form only when you submit the free architectural drawing request. This information is used to respond to your request and provide project support.

Permissions and browsing data

The extension does not read browsing history, inspect unrelated websites, sell personal information or serve advertising. Access to trimwallhaus.com is used only for live pricing, product links and the customer-initiated contact form.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Trim Wall Haus contact page.
